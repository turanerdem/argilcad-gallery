These terms apply when you submit a model to the ArgilCAD Gallery from inside
ArgilCAD ("Share to gallery"). "We" is Argil Design; "the gallery" is this
repository, the gallery pages on argildesign.com, and ArgilCAD's own Explore
view.

## 1. You keep your copyright

Your model stays yours. These terms give us a licence to it; they transfer
nothing.

## 2. What you give us

You grant Argil Design a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual
licence to:

- host, store and copy your submission, and run its code;
- make pictures, previews, 3D files and animations of it;
- show it in the gallery, in ArgilCAD and in Argil Design's own promotion
  (website, social media, videos), with credit to you;
- publish it under the licence you chose (section 3);
- include it in datasets that Argil Design makes, and use those datasets,
  including to train and evaluate AI models.

## 3. The licence you choose for everyone

You choose one licence for the public from the list in
[LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) — by default CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. When you remix a
gallery model, the original's licence may decide yours (LICENSE.md, "remix").

## 4. AI training

The gallery is public and openly readable, **including by AI systems that
learn from it**. Do not submit anything you do not want read that way.

## 5. Your edit history — only if you say so

If, and only if, you tick the box for it, your submission also carries the
model's edit history from ArgilCAD: the prompts, the intermediate code and
the errors along the way. That history:

- is **never published** and never enters this repository;
- is stored by Argil Design (on Google Cloud Firestore) and used only to
  improve ArgilCAD and to train and evaluate Argil Design's own AI models;
- can be withdrawn at any time by writing to support@argildesign.com, after
  which it is deleted and left out of every later dataset.

## 6. What you promise

- The model is your own work, or you have the right to submit it under these
  terms and the licence you chose.
- It follows the content policy in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md): no
  weapons or weapon parts, no brands, logos or protected designs you do not
  own, no personal data.
- Its text (title, description, the prompt if you share it) contains nothing
  you need to keep private.

## 7. Review, and no obligation to publish

Every submission is reviewed by Argil Design before it appears anywhere. We
may decline or remove a submission at our discretion; we will tell you why
when we decline one.

## 8. Removal

You can withdraw a submission still under review at any time from ArgilCAD.
To remove a published one, write to support@argildesign.com with its `id`: it
leaves the gallery and every later dataset. Copies already made by others,
and the history of this git repository, cannot be recalled — which is why the
gallery shows you this before you submit.

## 9. No warranty

The gallery and its contents are provided as they are, without warranty. A
model is not checked for fitness for any purpose; print and use it at your
own risk.
